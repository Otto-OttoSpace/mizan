RTL Arena

How well do AI coding tools actually handle Arabic?

A small, reproducible benchmark of Cursor, Claude, Copilot, v0 and Lovable on right-to-left UI — graded automatically by the Otto linters. Everyone measures speed and correctness in English; nobody measures whether the AI-built web works for a fifth of the planet.

Results are being collected in the open. Want your tool scored (or want to help)? Run the 15 prompts and open a PR.

Method

  1. Give each tool the same 15 Arabic/RTL prompts.
  2. Save its output; grade it: node score.js outputs
  3. The graders are rtlint + arabitype + dls-check — objective, reproducible.
